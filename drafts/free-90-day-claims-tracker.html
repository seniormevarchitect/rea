Free Provider Readiness Starter Pack

Free 90-day claims tracker and “Am I likely affected?” self-check for small providers

From 1 December 2026, claims will need to be made within 90 days of the support being delivered, by whoever claims (you, the plan manager or the participant). Until now the limit was 2 years. This free pack is a way to keep track of your supports and what's needed to get them paid, and to take an honest first look at whether the registration changes are likely to affect you.

Official sources set the 90-day limit to start on 1 Dec 2026; the Government's Explanatory Memorandum says the start was delayed to allow time for claims older than 90 days to be made. To be safe, make sure claims for older supports are lodged by whoever claims before 1 December 2026. Not legal advice. As at 2 November 2026, no transitional rules have been made for this change.

The 90-day claims tracker

One row per support. Fill in your participant code, “Plan management” and the “Support date”. The tracker fills in “Who usually claims (auto)” (you for NDIA-managed plans, the plan manager, or the participant if they self-manage) and a “Claim-by date (guide only)”, which is the support date plus 89 days, so the support day counts as day 1. If this falls on a weekend or public holiday, the legal deadline may move to the next business day (Acts Interpretation Act s36(2)). Don't rely on it. For older supports, the guide date stops at 30 November 2026. The “Days left (auto)” and “Alert (auto)” cells change colour as that date gets close. There are also columns for “Submission date”, “Reference / confirmation”, “Follow-up needed” and “Where the record is kept”. It's a planning aid. It doesn't calculate a personalised legal deadline.

The spreadsheet in this repository is starter-pack/claims-tracker-90-day.xlsx. This page does not email it to anyone.

The checklist

Short steps for claims, record keeping, registration and enrolment. For example: list every support you've delivered but haven't been paid for, oldest first, and set up a filing system for your records. See the checklist draft.
