Readiness checklist

Short steps for claims, record keeping, registration and enrolment. Work top to bottom. If a step doesn't apply, write why in your own words.

Claims

  • List every support you've delivered but haven't been paid for, oldest first.
  • Add one row per support in the 90-day claims tracker, with the support date and plan management type.
  • Invoice soon after each support, weekly or fortnightly if you can, so whoever claims still has time.

Record keeping

  • Set up a filing system for your records.
  • For each support, note where the record is kept. The tracker has a column for that.
  • From claims made on or after 27 August 2026, plan to keep the provider's claim and support records for 7 years from the day the claim is made, whoever makes the claim.

Registration and enrolment

  • Answer the self-check. Treat “not sure” as a yes.
  • Write down which supports you deliver, in your own words. Don't copy a policy.
  • If you later compare audit quotes, line up more than one quote from approved quality auditors: what's included, how many days, travel, and follow-up visits. Write the figures down before you sign anything.
  • Enrolment with the NDIA, from 1 July 2027 for most providers, is separate from registration. Don't treat them as the same step.
