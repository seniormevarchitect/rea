Am I likely affected?

As at 2 November 2026 the official list of high risk supports hasn't been published. If you're not sure, treat the answer as a yes. This is not an eligibility test, a legal assessment or an official registration decision, and it doesn't list every case where registration is already required.

  1. Are you a sole trader or small provider delivering supports funded under participants' plans?
  2. Do you deliver personal care or daily living supports?
  3. Do you deliver supports in closed settings or supported independent living (SIL), support participants whose funding is NDIA-managed, or run an NDIS digital platform?
  4. Are you unsure whether your supports are in scope?
  5. Could you find the records for every support you've been paid for since 27 August 2026?

Answered yes or not sure to any question? Work through the readiness checklist and the tracker. Answered no to all of them? That doesn't mean no rules apply. Check again when the official list comes out.
