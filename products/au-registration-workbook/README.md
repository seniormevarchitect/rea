# Registration Decision Workbook

`outline-v2.md` was the source the 2 November funnel used for the ten promised parts. That file is not in this repository, and it is not reconstructed here.

The public draft promises these parts, and only these, once you approve the outline:

1. Guided decision tree with three results: “Rules may already apply today: check with the Commission”, “likely affected”, or “to verify”.
2. Plain explainer of registration versus NDIA enrolment.
3. Audit cost planner for up to three quotes from approved quality auditors.
4. Evidence register with prompts, written in the provider's own words.
5. Worker screening and training registers.
6. Risk register.
7. Incident and complaints registers.
8. Continuous improvement register.
9. 2026–2030 planning calendar.
10. How-to guide.

Promised format: a fillable PDF plus a companion spreadsheet. Delivery in the draft copy is 15 February 2027. Do not open the pre-order until the outline is approved.

Page drafts live in `drafts/`. They are not published.
