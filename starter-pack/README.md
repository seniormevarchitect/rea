# 90-day claims tracker

`claims-tracker-90-day.xlsx` is generated by `build_tracker.py`. Cream cells are inputs. Blue cells are formulas.

Guide date = support date + 89 days (the support day counts as day 1). Supports before 1 December 2026 are capped at 30 November 2026. The sheet does not move a guide date off a weekend.

Settings cell B1 is the safety buffer, in days. The rebuilt default is 14 because the 01:53 file's buffer was not in this workspace. Days left and Alert use the target date (guide date minus the buffer) and go blank once a submission date is entered.

The 01:53 note said colour lived only on columns K and M. Those letters could not be matched to Days left and Alert without the original file. In this rebuild, colour is only on Days left (column G) and Alert (column I).

Delete the three EXAMPLE rows before entering real supports.
